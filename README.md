
ReadSpeed is a minimalist, web-based speed reading tool designed to help you consume text faster and more efficiently. By utilizing Rapid Serial Visual Presentation (RSVP), ReadSpeed eliminates the time-consuming process of moving your eyes across a page, allowing you to focus on one word at a time at a pace that suits your cognitive processing speed.

How It Works
Traditional reading involves "scanning" and "fixation," where your eyes jump from word to word. ReadSpeed uses RSVP technology to present words in a fixed position, reducing "subvocalization" and eye strain while significantly increasing your Words Per Minute (WPM).

By concentrating your focus on a single focal point, you can train your brain to recognize and process words without the mechanical delay of eye movement.

Key Features
Adjustable Velocity: Easily change the Words Per Minute (WPM) to match your comfort level, whether you are a beginner or an advanced speed reader.

Custom Text Input: Paste any article, essay, or book chapter directly into the reader.

Minimalist UI: A clean, distraction-free interface designed to keep your focus entirely on the content.

Play/Pause Controls: Take breaks or restart segments at your own pace.

Getting Started
Navigate to the live application: readspeed.shawnmirman.com

Paste the text you wish to read into the input field.

Set your desired WPM.

Press Start and focus on the center of the display area.
