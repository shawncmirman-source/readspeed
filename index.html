The Science Behind ReadSpeed

ReadSpeed utilizes RSVP (Rapid Serial Visual Presentation). By highlighting the Optimal Recognition Point (ORP)—usually slightly to the left of a word's center—we trigger the brain to recognize the word shape instantly, bypassing the need to "sub-vocalize" every individual letter.

Rapid Serial Visual Presentation (RSVP)

Text is displayed word-by-word in a fixed focal position. This eliminates the time spent moving your eyes from word to word (saccades).
